COLE PROFESSION PLANNER
=======================

Cole Profession Planner is a profession leveling route calculator for the
official World of Warcraft: Burning Crusade Classic Anniversary client 2.5.6.

FEATURES
--------

- Price-aware profession routes with orange/yellow/green skill-up chances.
- Quantity-aware Auctionator pricing and final shopping lists.
- Optional current-character and alt bag/bank inventory deductions.
- Intermediate crafts, item conversions, and profession requirements.
- Trainer, vendor, reputation, quest, and mob recipe sources.
- Map and optional TomTom navigation for the selected source.
- Automatic learned-profession skill and Anniversary phase detection.

INSTALLATION
------------

Place the ColeProfessionPlanner folder in:

World of Warcraft/_anniversary_/Interface/AddOns/

Open the planner with /cole, /cpp, or its minimap button.

OPTIONAL INTEGRATIONS
---------------------

Auctionator, TradeSkillMaster, TradeSkillMaster_AppHelper, Auctioneer, TomTom,
Baganator, Syndicator, Bagnon, and BagBrother are detected automatically. None
is required for the addon to load.

PRIVACY
-------

The addon does not transmit personal or gameplay data outside World of
Warcraft. Settings and caches remain in SavedVariables. Auction searches are
initiated by the player through installed auction addons.

This is an unofficial fan-made addon and is not affiliated with or endorsed by
Blizzard Entertainment.
