COLE PROFESSION PLANNER CHANGELOG
=================================

0.9.17-tbc
----------

- Stopped gray, green and yellow mandatory Runed Rod crafts from being counted as guaranteed skill points.
- Kept each missing rod as a required one-craft prerequisite without shifting the real leveling range.
- Continued to count an orange mandatory rod as one guaranteed skill point.
- Added explicit same-skill craft labels and preserved skill-gain state in saved routes.

0.9.16-tbc
----------

- Rebuilt leveling rows with fixed text bounds so recipe details cannot overlap adjacent rows.
- Limited recipe names to one line and material summaries to two lines; full details remain in the tooltip.
- Split cost and route flags into separate single-line fields.
- Increased row spacing and reduced visible route rows to seven for consistent vertical rhythm.

0.9.15-tbc
----------

- Added a no-travel route policy that skips unknown vendor recipes by default.
- Added a Vendor trips option for players who want cheaper routes that require travel.
- Listed skipped vendor recipes as alternatives with estimated savings and mapped sources.
- Added a one-click route switch between no-travel and vendor-trip plans in Recipe options.
- Kept learned recipes, recipes already in inventory and confirmed auction copies usable without vendor travel.

0.9.14-tbc
----------

- Stopped unknown seasonal recipes from entering leveling routes until the character has learned them.
- Required fresh in-game stock confirmation before using limited vendor recipes such as Schematic: Red Firework.
- Added automatic limited-recipe stock scans when opening or updating the relevant merchant window.
- Added a Recipe options window for mapped vendors, auction alternatives, savings and blocked seasonal acquisitions.
- Added explicit Buy recipe, Learn recipe and Train recipe requirements to route rows and tooltips.
- Corrected Large Blue Rocket to its Lunar Festival, level 35, 5 Coins of Ancestry, Bind-on-Pickup acquisition.

0.9.13-tbc
----------

- Saved the latest successful route separately for each profession and restored it after reloads or profession switches.
- Preserved the purchased-material shopping list, route steps, costs and craft-time estimates in a compact SavedVariables snapshot.
- Stopped planning blocks at newly available recipe thresholds before reevaluating the best recipe.
- Kept five-point blocks as the maximum block size instead of crossing a better recipe unlock by one skill point.

0.9.12-tbc
----------

- Added mandatory one-craft progression steps for the full Runed Rod chain.
- Reserved exactly one skill point for each required rod instead of a five-point planning block.
- Reused intermediate items crafted earlier in the route, including Fine Leather Belt for Dark Leather Belt.
- Separated crafted intermediates from bag and bank inventory in route labels and material totals.
- Excluded completed lower rod ranks from range price refreshes when a higher rod is already available.

0.9.11-tbc
----------

- Corrected TBC First Aid manual sources for Heavy Silk, Mageweave and Netherweave bandages.
- Removed WotLK-only trainer acquisitions from the TBC Anniversary recipe database.
- Limited Heavy Mageweave and Runecloth bandage training to the faction trauma surgeons used after Triage.

0.9.10-tbc
----------

- Registered the minimap launcher through LibDataBroker and LibDBIcon when available.
- Made the icon participate in SexyMap fading and standard minimap-button management.
- Preserved the lightweight custom button as a fallback when shared libraries are unavailable.

0.9.9-tbc
---------

- Added Fast route mode for high skill-up chance and Economy mode for lowest expected material cost.
- Required a fresh Auctionator result with an available listing before using an unknown auction recipe.
- Recorded zero-result Auctionator searches so stale recipe prices cannot re-enter a route.
- Rebuilt the planner as a three-column workbench with settings, route and shopping list visible together.
- Recalculated the route automatically when an Auctionator quantity scan finishes.

0.9.8-tbc
---------

- Prevented profession routes from returning to an already retired recipe when a new usable option exists.
- Required reliable quantity prices and at least 15% savings before upgrading full leather tiers for shopping.
- Added per-route-step shopping materials after bag and intermediate-output allocation.
- Added per-step crafting time using client cast times with a marked fallback estimate.

0.9.7-tbc
---------

- Planned profession routes in five-skill blocks instead of committing one point at a time.
- Required at least 12% estimated savings before switching away from the current recipe.

0.9.6-tbc
---------

- Limited price refreshes to recipes usable inside the selected skill range.
- Prevented missing prices for optional recipes from blocking Leatherworking routes.
- Excluded fixed-price profession vendor supplies from Auctionator searches and marked them in shopping lists.
- Added automatic version bumps and protected existing release archives from being overwritten.

0.9.5-tbc
---------

- Added profession-specific Cole artwork for the project and minimap icon.
- Added quantity-aware auction pricing and full candidate-material refreshes.
- Added shopping-list creation and Auctionator material searches.
- Added current-character and alt bag/bank inventory support.
- Added profession-gated intermediate crafts and alternative recipe pricing.
- Added recipe source selection with map and optional TomTom navigation.
- Added automatic profession skill and TBC Anniversary phase detection.
- Added CurseForge-style package metadata, documentation, and validation.
